# Conflict Resolution Scenarios

1. **User changes audience:** current explicit request starts a decision-change flow; explain impacts and retain the prior decision history.
2. **Two supplier quotes differ:** preserve both and compare date, specification, quantity, tax, shipping, and terms; don't average without basis.
3. **Market source conflicts with old report:** compare source date, scope, method, and authority; retain both and mark dependent analysis for review.
4. **Generic marketing advice conflicts with a confirmed strategy:** use the confirmed strategy until the user changes it; surface tradeoffs.
5. **Creative packaging conflicts with safety requirement:** flag the applicable safety constraint and redesign; ordinary creative preference cannot override it.

Pass when the conflict and consequence are visible and no source or decision is silently discarded.
