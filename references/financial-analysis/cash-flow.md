# Cash-Flow Planning

Build a period-by-period cash schedule from opening cash, receipts, payments, financing, and closing cash. Separate profit recognition from cash timing. Include deposits, inventory, payment delays, tax obligations, refunds, capital purchases, and seasonality where relevant.

State timing assumptions and distinguish committed cash from forecast cash. Identify the lowest cash balance, funding gap, and date range under each scenario. Do not invent financing availability. If inputs are incomplete, provide a template and identify the variables needed rather than presenting a precise runway.
