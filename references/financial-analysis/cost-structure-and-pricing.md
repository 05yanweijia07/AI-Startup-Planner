# Cost Structure and Pricing

## Cost model

For each cost record amount, currency, unit or period, source, date, volume tier, tax/shipping inclusion, and whether fixed, variable, one-time, or step-based. Separate cash costs from non-cash accounting treatment where relevant. Include channel fees, packaging, fulfillment, returns, waste, payment costs, and labor when applicable.

## Pricing workflow

1. Define the offer unit and net price basis.
2. Calculate per-unit variable cost and contribution for each material channel.
3. Compare customer value and alternatives using evidence; cost-plus is a floor/reference, not proof of willingness to pay.
4. Check price against positioning, channel margin, capacity, and break-even.
5. Present testable price options and their assumptions; don't claim a price is validated without observed evidence.
