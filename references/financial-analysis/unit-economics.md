# Unit Economics

Define the unit first: one product, order, subscriber-month, service visit, or another explicit unit. Keep price and costs on the same unit and channel.

Core measures may include net revenue per unit, variable cost per unit, contribution per unit, contribution margin, acquisition cost, repeat rate, and lifetime value. Include a metric only when its inputs and observation period are defined. Do not divide broad marketing spend by an unrelated customer count and label it CAC; do not forecast lifetime value from an unsupported retention assumption.

Show formulas and input sources. Use cohorts or scenario ranges when behavior varies. State which costs are excluded and how scaling may change them.
