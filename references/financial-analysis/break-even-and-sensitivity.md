# Break-Even and Sensitivity Analysis

Use the formulas in `core-workflow.md` only when units and periods align and contribution is positive. For multiple products, show the assumed sales mix and calculate weighted contribution; explain that a mix shift changes the result.

Sensitivity steps:

1. Identify the few assumptions that most affect viability (price, volume, variable cost, fixed cost, conversion, timing).
2. Set plausible ranges from evidence or label them as illustrative.
3. Change assumptions transparently and show the result for each scenario.
4. Check capacity, cash timing, seasonality, and constraints that a simple break-even formula omits.
5. Convert the largest uncertainty into a validation action.

Never present the break-even point as a sales forecast or guarantee. If contribution is zero or negative, state that increasing volume alone does not reach break-even under the stated model.
