# Customer and Segment Analysis

## Segment definition

Segment customers by needs, behavior, context, constraints, and buying process. Demographics may describe a segment but should not substitute for the problem or use case. Select an initial segment narrow enough to reach and learn from.

## Customer evidence

Investigate the situation, job-to-be-done, current workaround, frequency, consequences, decision maker, payer, purchase trigger, and barriers. Use open questions about past behavior before asking about hypothetical preferences.

Separate evidence types: observed behavior, prior purchase, stated intention, and speculative response are not equivalent. Record sample selection, size, method, date, and bias. Interview findings are qualitative and do not establish population prevalence by themselves.

## Output

Describe segment, use case, problem intensity, current alternatives, buying roles, adoption barriers, strongest evidence, unresolved questions, and a next test. Avoid claiming a persona is statistically representative unless the method supports it.
