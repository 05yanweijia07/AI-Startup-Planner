# Market Sizing

## Method

1. Define offer, customer, geography, period, and what counts as a purchase.
2. Estimate addressable demand using a transparent top-down or bottom-up method, preferably cross-checking both.
3. Separate total market, serviceable market, and near-term reachable market; explain filters and exclusions.
4. Use comparable units, currency, dates, and customer definitions. Avoid adding overlapping segments.
5. Show inputs, formula, source, uncertainty range, and sensitivity to major assumptions.

### Bottom-up pattern

Potential buyers in defined scope × plausible purchase frequency × average net spend = annual opportunity, adjusted for reach, capacity, and conversion only when those assumptions have a basis.

### Top-down pattern

Start from a cited, relevant category value and apply explicit scope filters. Explain why each filter is defensible and avoid presenting the result as obtainable revenue.

## Quality checks

Distinguish market size from the venture's sales forecast. Reject unsupported precision, incompatible units, duplicated categories, and estimates built from unrelated industry totals. If reliable inputs are missing, provide a calculation framework and data needs rather than a fabricated number.
