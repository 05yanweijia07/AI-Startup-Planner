# Competitor and Substitute Analysis

## Comparison method

Define the customer problem and comparison set first. Include direct competitors, indirect substitutes, in-house solutions, and doing nothing where relevant. Compare like-for-like offers on customer, job, product scope, price basis, channel, geography, evidence of traction, and date.

Use current accessible sources and label estimates. Distinguish a competitor's stated positioning from independently verified performance. Do not infer weakness from missing public information.

## Synthesis

Identify unmet needs, parity requirements, differentiation hypotheses, entry barriers, and implications for positioning or validation. A feature comparison alone is not a strategy. Cite source and scope for material claims and preserve unknown fields as unknown.
