# Market Research Core Workflow

## Module metadata

- **Module ID:** MKT-001
- **Category:** Market research
- **Version:** 1.0.0-draft
- **Status:** Initial core Reference
- **Triggers:** Market sizing, market trends, customer research, competitor analysis, demand validation
- **Exclusions:** Copy editing or unrelated operating questions
- **Dependencies:** Portable control rules; project profile and evidence registry when available

## Purpose

Build a decision-useful view of a defined market and its customers. Research should reduce a named uncertainty, not accumulate facts without a decision in view.

## Required inputs

Identify the offer or problem, geography, customer group, time period, project stage, and decision the research should inform. If these are unknown, state assumptions or ask only for the scope choices that materially change the result.

## Workflow

1. **Frame the question.** State the decision, market boundary, geography, segment, and period. Separate the founder's hypothesis from the research question.
2. **Map the market.** Define substitutes and alternatives. Size only the addressable scope relevant to the decision; distinguish total, serviceable, and realistically reachable demand when estimates are possible.
3. **Gather evidence.** Prefer primary and authoritative sources for current facts. Record publisher, URL or file, date, scope, method, and limitations. Use customer interviews, observation, or tests for unmet-need claims when feasible.
4. **Analyze customers.** Describe jobs, pains, current alternatives, buying context, willingness-to-pay evidence, and adoption barriers. Do not treat broad demographic description as proof of demand.
5. **Analyze competitors and substitutes.** Compare customer, offer, price basis, channel, positioning, strengths, and gaps using like-for-like information. Include the option to do nothing where relevant.
6. **Synthesize.** Present what is supported, contradicted, unknown, and inferred. Explain implications for the decision and identify the smallest next validation step.

## Output

Provide an executive finding, scope and method, customer/market/competitor evidence, source list, limitations, unresolved assumptions, and decision implications. Include ranges and calculation logic for market estimates; avoid unsupported single-point precision.

## Quality and risk checks

- A source supports only the claim, population, geography, and period it actually covers.
- Do not sum overlapping market categories or equate industry revenue with reachable demand.
- Separate published market data, user-provided information, estimates, and inference.
- Disclose missing, stale, paywalled, or non-comparable evidence.
- Do not claim customer validation from desk research alone.

## Integration

Record source-backed claims in the Evidence Registry when persistence is available. Send customer needs and uncertainty to Business Strategy and Product & Operations. Pass only stated assumptions and ranges to Financial Analysis.
