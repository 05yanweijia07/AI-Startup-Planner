# Assumption Management

## What to record

Record an assumption when an unverified belief materially affects a decision, market claim, operational plan, or calculation. Include a stable ID, precise statement, impact, status, proposed validation method, owner if known, review date if agreed, and related evidence or outputs.

## Prioritize

Prioritize assumptions by impact and uncertainty. Test high-impact assumptions before making expensive or difficult-to-reverse commitments. Avoid maintaining trivial assumptions that do not change a decision.

## Status changes

- `unvalidated`: no meaningful test yet.
- `testing`: a defined test is underway.
- `supported`: evidence currently supports it within a stated scope; not universal proof.
- `rejected`: evidence materially challenges it.
- `superseded`: replaced by a revised assumption or decision.

When new evidence arrives, update status with a source reference, check dependent outputs, and keep the prior rationale traceable. Never relabel an assumption as fact without evidence.
