# Business Model Canvas

Use the nine blocks as a consistency map, not a checklist to fill with guesses:

1. Customer segments
2. Value propositions
3. Channels
4. Customer relationships
5. Revenue streams
6. Key resources
7. Key activities
8. Key partnerships
9. Cost structure

For each block, label evidence-backed facts, decisions, and assumptions. Trace dependencies between value proposition, segment, channel, price/revenue, delivery resources, and costs. Identify the riskiest unresolved fit and a test. If a block is unknown, record the question rather than filling it with generic language.
