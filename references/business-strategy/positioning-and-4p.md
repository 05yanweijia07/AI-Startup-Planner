# Positioning and Marketing Mix

## Positioning

State the target customer and context, relevant alternative, distinctive benefit, and reason to believe. Check that the statement is understandable, relevant, differentiated within the real comparison set, and supportable by evidence. Mark unproven claims as hypotheses.

## 4P decisions

- **Product:** customer outcome, scope, quality, service, packaging, and exclusions.
- **Price:** price metric, range, willingness-to-pay evidence, margin constraints, and discount policy.
- **Place:** channel reach, economics, control, fulfillment, and customer access.
- **Promotion:** message, audience, channel, cost, and measurable learning objective.

Assess 4Ps as a coherent set. Every tactic should connect to a customer segment and business objective; financial and operational constraints must be checked before recommending rollout.
