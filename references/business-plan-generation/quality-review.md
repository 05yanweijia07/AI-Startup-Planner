# Business Plan Quality Review

Review the draft for:

- **Traceability:** each material factual claim maps to evidence, a project record, or a labeled assumption.
- **Strategic fit:** customer, problem, offer, channel, and revenue logic align.
- **Operational fit:** capacity, sourcing, delivery, and capability claims are plausible and scoped.
- **Financial consistency:** currency, unit, period, price, volume, cost, and scenario labels agree.
- **Gate honesty:** unresolved or unpassed gates are visible; no projection is described as actual traction.
- **Audience fit:** purpose, level of detail, terminology, and requested format are respected.
- **Risk transparency:** major unknowns and next validation steps are included.

Classify findings as blocking, material, or editorial. Correct inconsistencies before polishing language. A polished document is not evidence that the venture is validated.
