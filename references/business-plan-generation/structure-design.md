# Business Plan Structure Design

Choose structure for audience and decision, not by habit. A concise plan may include:

1. Executive summary (written after the analysis)
2. Customer problem and target segment
3. Offer and value proposition
4. Market scope and evidence
5. Business model and go-to-market
6. Product delivery and operations
7. Team and capability gaps
8. Financial assumptions and scenarios
9. Risks, milestones, and validation plan
10. Sources and appendices

For an internal plan, emphasize assumptions, experiments, owners, and decision dates. For a lender or investor audience, match the requested format without inventing traction, collateral, approvals, or funding commitments. Omit sections that do not serve the purpose; call out material missing data.
