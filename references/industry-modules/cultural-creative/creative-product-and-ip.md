# Cultural and Creative Product and IP Planning

## Scope

Use when cultural meaning, creative content, character/IP, storytelling, collectible elements, or experience design materially shape the offer. Do not treat cultural motifs as interchangeable decoration.

## Planning workflow

1. State the intended audience, cultural context, customer use, and meaning the product should convey.
2. Identify provenance and permissions for text, imagery, designs, folklore adaptations, names, music, or commissioned work.
3. Separate original work, licensed material, public-domain source material, and user-created content; document ownership and allowed use.
4. Design an authentic and respectful customer experience, checking local community context and avoiding unsupported historical claims.
5. Test comprehension, emotional response, usability, and willingness to pay with the intended audience.
6. Check reproducibility, production cost, packaging, version control, and consistency as the creative concept scales.

## Risk and output

Flag intellectual-property ownership, licensing, attribution, cultural sensitivity, and commercialization questions for qualified review where needed. Provide concept choices, provenance/permission checklist, audience test, production implications, and unresolved risks. Do not give a legal clearance conclusion.
