# Food and Beverage Product Operations

## Scope

Use for food-related product definition, sourcing, production, packaging, storage, handling, and delivery. Specific legal requirements depend on jurisdiction, product, process, and sales channel; verify current authoritative rules for the actual location before operational decisions.

## Planning checklist

- Define ingredients, recipe/specification, portion, shelf-life target, storage, and intended consumption context.
- Map supplier identity, ingredient origin, minimums, quote date, substitutions, lead time, quality documentation, and continuity risk.
- Map production steps, equipment, labor, yield, waste, batch size, capacity, and quality checks.
- Separate food-contact packaging, gift packaging, shipping materials, labels, and protection; identify which need qualified compliance review.
- Model spoilage, breakage, returns, temperature or handling constraints, and delivery timing where relevant.
- Identify traceability, sanitation, allergen, labeling, licensing, and inspection questions for local professional/official review; do not assert compliance without verified evidence.

## Output

Provide a product specification, source/quote comparison, process and capacity assumptions, cost inputs for Financial Analysis, material safety/compliance questions, and a validation checklist. Distinguish an illustrative process from verified production capability.
