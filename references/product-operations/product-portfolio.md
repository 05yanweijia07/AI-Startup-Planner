# Product Portfolio and Offer Design

Describe each offer by target customer, job, core outcome, format, price basis, key components, service level, and exclusions. Group variants only when they share meaningful customer and operating logic. Evaluate portfolio complexity against sourcing, production, inventory, customer choice, and margin.

Prioritize additions by customer value, strategic fit, feasibility, contribution, and learning value. State evidence and uncertainty. Do not add features or SKUs solely to make a plan appear comprehensive.
