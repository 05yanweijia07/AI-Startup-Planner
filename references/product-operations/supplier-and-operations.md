# Supplier and Operations Planning

## Supplier comparison

Compare specification fit, minimum order, unit and setup cost, lead time, quality controls, capacity, payment terms, shipping, returns, continuity, and evidence source/date. Normalize quote quantities and included services before comparison. Treat a quote as time- and condition-bound.

## Operating process

Map order or service intake through delivery and support. Identify owner, capacity, bottleneck, quality checkpoint, failure/rework path, and customer communication at each critical step. Estimate throughput and service level from explicit constraints.

For regulated or safety-sensitive activity, identify jurisdiction-specific questions and qualified review needs; do not declare compliance from a generic checklist.
