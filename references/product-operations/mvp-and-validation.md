# MVP and Validation Experiments

## MVP scope

Define the riskiest customer or delivery assumption, the minimum offer needed to test it, what is excluded, and the learning objective. An MVP is the smallest credible experiment, not necessarily a reduced production product.

## Experiment card

Record: assumption ID; hypothesis; target participant; method; sample/recruitment plan; metric; decision threshold; duration; cost; ethical/privacy/safety considerations; and follow-up for positive, mixed, or negative results.

Prefer behavior or credible commitment over compliments. Use a threshold before seeing results to reduce hindsight bias. State sample limitations and do not generalize beyond them. Update the assumption and evidence registries after the test.
